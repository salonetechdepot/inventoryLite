# BIVA — Training Guide (Step by Step, v1)

**Audience:** New cashier or shop staff  
**Duration:** ~45–60 minutes hands-on  
**Trainer:** Shop owner or supervisor

Add screenshots from your shop device when turning this into a printed booklet.

---

## Module 1 — Get started (10 min)

### 1.1 Open the app

1. Open Chrome on phone/tablet.
2. Go to your BIVA link.
3. **Add to Home Screen** when prompted (recommended).
4. Open BIVA from home screen icon.

### 1.2 Sign in

1. Enter your phone/email.
2. Enter the **OTP code** you receive.
3. You should see **Home** with quick links.

### 1.3 Learn the bottom bar

- **Home** — dashboard
- **Products** — inventory list
- **Sell** — cash register
- **History** — past sales
- **Returns** — return receipts
- **Account** — settings, day close, credit book *(online only)*

**Practice:** Tap each tab once and return to Home.

---

## Module 2 — Your first sale (15 min)

### 2.1 Add items to cart

1. Tap **Sell**.
2. Tap a product (e.g. Rice).
3. See it in the cart at the bottom.
4. Tap another product to add more.

### 2.2 Change quantity

- Use **+ / −** on cart line to adjust quantity.

### 2.3 Search instead of scroll

1. Tap the **search box** at the top.
2. Type part of the product name.
3. Tap the product from results.

### 2.4 Checkout and pay

1. Tap **Checkout** (or cart button).
2. Confirm total in **NLe**.
3. Enter **amount customer paid**.
4. Tap **Complete sale** / **Confirm**.

### 2.5 Receipt

1. Receipt popup appears.
2. Tap **Print** → choose printer → print.
3. Tap **Done**.

**Practice:** Make 2 test sales with trainer watching.

---

## Module 3 — Scanning & barcodes (10 min)

### 3.1 Scan at checkout

1. On **Sell**, tap **scan icon** (if enabled in POS settings).
2. Point camera at product barcode.
3. Product should jump into cart.

**Important:** Scanner matches **exact scan code** stored in BIVA — not product name.

### 3.2 When scan fails

- Product not in system → add under Products first.
- No barcode on item → search by name.
- Wrong barcode → check label matches product in app.

---

## Module 4 — Products & labels (10 min)

### 4.1 Find a product

1. **Products** tab.
2. Scroll or search.

### 4.2 Quick stock check

- Read **“X in stock”** badge on each card.

### 4.3 Print a shelf label

1. Find product with scan code.
2. Tap **tag icon** on product card.
3. Choose **Barcode** or **QR code**.
4. Wait for preview image.
5. Tap **Print label**.
6. In print dialog: 58mm, no margins, no headers, 100% scale.

**Practice:** Print one label and scan it on Sell screen.

---

## Module 5 — Returns (10 min)

### 5.1 Return from a past sale

1. **History** → tap a sale.
2. Tap **Return** (or similar action).
3. Sell opens in **return mode**.
4. Select item and quantity to return.
5. Complete return.

### 5.2 Check return recorded

1. **Returns** tab.
2. Open return receipt.
3. Confirm it shows **linked original sale**.

**Practice:** Return 1 item from a training sale.

---

## Module 6 — Offline & credit (5 min)

### 6.1 Offline sale (demo)

Trainer turns off WiFi briefly:

1. Make a small sale anyway.
2. Notice **“Saved offline”** message.
3. Turn WiFi back on — sale syncs automatically.

### 6.2 Credit sale (if shop uses credit)

1. At checkout, pay **less than total**.
2. Receipt shows **balance due**.
3. Owner finds customer under **Account → Credit book**.

---

## Module 7 — End of day (owner only, 5 min)

1. **Account → End-of-day close**.
2. Count cash in drawer.
3. Type counted amount.
4. Read variance line.
5. **Save** (internet required).
